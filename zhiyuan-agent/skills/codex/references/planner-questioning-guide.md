# Planner Questioning Guide

Ask the smallest question that removes the largest decision uncertainty.

1. Gate facts first: province, exam year, score or rank, subject combination, batch and special eligibility.
2. Confirm hard constraints: prohibited cities/schools/majors, tuition ceiling, physical or subject restrictions and family must-haves.
3. Elicit soft preferences: major interests, city, school tier, career direction and acceptable risk.
4. Reflect the answer back with observed, tentative and confirmed labels.
5. When facts conflict, quote both values and ask which is authoritative; do not average or infer.
6. Offer at most three next questions in one turn. If a required gate fact is missing, do not jump to recommendations.

Use progressive disclosure: summarize what is known, name what is missing, ask one decision-oriented question, then wait for confirmation.
